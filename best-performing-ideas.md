## Content Formats That Performed (by consensus)

All 5 interns independently converged on the same content angles:

1. **Counter-intuitive takes** — "Why X is actually bad", "The truth might disappoint you", "Here's what people miss"
2. **Corporate drama** — Executive firings, founder departures, rebrandings — high click-through
3. **Specific numbers + tight timeframes** — "in 30 Minutes", "in 48 Hours", "1.6 million agents"
4. **AI agent autonomy** — Clawdbot/Moltbot/OpenClaw framing; agents acting without prompts
5. **"Explainer on a viral thing"** — Moltbook, SaaSpocalypse, OpenClaw — new concepts with no existing content

## Hook Formulas (from "Best Hooks" spreadsheet in Drive)

The highest-signal hook patterns by awareness stage:

| Stage | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| No awareness | Intrigue/hidden problem | "The overlooked [factor] in your [context] that's draining your [resource]" |
| Problem aware | Pain acknowledgment | "Struggling with [problem]? Here's an alternative approach" |
| Solution aware | Benefit statement | "Achieve [result] in [timeframe] using [method]" |
| Brand aware | Social evidence | "[Number] people used [X] to solve [problem] in [timeframe]" |
| Most aware | Direct promotion | "Get [X]% off — available until [date]" |

**Key implementation rule:** Generate 50+ hook variations per content cycle and distribute across all awareness stages.
