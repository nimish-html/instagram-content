## Content Formats That Performed (by consensus)

Interns independently converged on the same content angles:

1. **Contrarian "Actually" takes** — "[Popular thing] isn't what you think." e.g. "Clawdbot executes perfectly… and that's exactly the problem." Stops the scroll by correcting a widely held belief; works well for reels.
2. **Corporate drama** — Executive firings, founder departures, rebrandings — high click-through.
3. **Specific numbers + tight timeframes** — "in 30 Minutes", "in 48 Hours", "1.6 million agents". Specificity signals credibility; drama drives shares.
4. **AI agent autonomy** — Clawdbot/Moltbot/OpenClaw framing; agents acting without prompts.
5. **"Here's what nobody's talking about"** — surface the overlooked angle on a trending topic (e.g. "None of these features are actually new"). Gives the audience a reason to share.
6. **Stakes-based hooks** — lead with consequence, not feature (e.g. "An AI agent founder lost control of his account — and the internet turned it into a $15M scam overnight").
7. **Counterintuitive one-liners** — "The real insight is X, not Y." Memorable takeaways get saved/reshared as quotes on Instagram.
8. **"Explainer on a viral thing"** — Moltbook, SaaSpocalypse, OpenClaw — new concepts with no existing content yet, so first-mover explainers win.

## Hook Formulas by Audience Awareness Stage

(From the "Best Hooks" reference in one submission — useful as a checklist when drafting new reels.)

| Stage | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| No awareness | Intrigue/hidden problem | "The overlooked [factor] in your [context] that's draining your [resource]" |
| Problem aware | Pain acknowledgment | "Struggling with [problem]? Here's an alternative approach" |
| Solution aware | Benefit statement | "Achieve [result] in [timeframe] using [method]" |
| Brand aware | Social proof | "[Number] people used [X] to solve [problem] in [timeframe]" |
| Most aware | Direct promotion | "Get [X]% off — available until [date]" |

**Implementation rule:** generate 50+ hook variations per content cycle, distributed across all five stages.
