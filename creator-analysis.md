# Best Performing Ideas from Content Creator Research

Source: Analysis of 5 intern assignment submissions (Feb 2026) recommending AI YouTube creators.
Each intern was asked to name creators who "explain how things work, not just report news."

---

## Top Creators by Cross-Submission Endorsement

### Tier 1 — Most Cited (3 mentions)

**Andrej Karpathy** | youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy  
Former Tesla AI Director, OpenAI founding member. Builds neural networks from scratch in code.
"Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" is the benchmark. Explains AI like a builder, not a marketer — videos age well.  
*Content angle for Instagram: demystify how models actually work, not what they can do.*

### Tier 2 — Strong Consensus (2 mentions each)

**AI Explained** | youtube.com/@aiexplained-official  
Reads original research papers, cites sources, breaks down benchmarks vs. marketing claims.
Gold standard for "here's what the paper actually says" content.  
*Content angle: debunk hype with data. "X claims state-of-the-art — here's what the benchmark actually shows."*

**Varun Mayya** | youtube.com/@VarunMayya  
Uses AI inside his own business to optimize the content pipeline, then teaches what worked.
Not surface-level — shows real leverage, provides that as a service to brands.  
*Content angle: behind-the-scenes of using AI in a real business. Practical over theoretical.*

**Yannic Kilcher** | youtube.com/@YannicKilcher  
Paper walkthroughs: explains why something works, not just that it exists.
Also covers societal implications — adds context beyond pure capability.  
*Content angle: "I read the paper so you don't have to." Include the surprising bit buried in section 4.*

**Two Minute Papers** | youtube.com/@TwoMinutePapers  
Short, high-enthusiasm breakdowns of new AI research. Grounded — no math overload, no hype.  
*Content angle: fast-format research highlights. One paper, one idea, one takeaway.*

## Best-Performing Content Ideas (by pattern frequency across submissions)

These angles appeared most consistently across the video titles and scripts submitted:

### 1. Contrarian "Actually" Takes
Format: "[Popular thing] isn't what you think."
Examples from submissions:
- "People are calling Clawdbot the future of AI… but the truth might disappoint you."
- "Clawdbot executes perfectly… and that's exactly the problem."
- "Clawdbot doesn't really think — it executes."

Why it works: stops the scroll by promising to correct a widely held belief. Works well for reels.

### 2. Specific Numbers + Drama
Format: "[Specific number] + [unexpected event] in [short time]"
Examples from submissions:
- "Half of Elon Musk's xAI Founders Just Quit in 48 Hours"
- "1.6 million AI agents are talking to each other right now—and nobody knows what they're saying."
- "Anthropic Just Released a Legal AI Tool — Why the Market Reacted So Fast"

Why it works: specificity signals credibility; drama drives shares.

### 3. "Here's What Nobody's Talking About"
Format: Surface the overlooked angle on a trending topic.
Examples:
- "But here's what nobody's talking about: the agents on Moltbook aren't just posting memes."
- "Here's the part most people don't realise — none of these features are actually new."
- "What the Cisco AI Summit Reveals About Where AI Is Actually Being Used"

Why it works: gives the audience a reason to share ("I learned something others don't know").

### 4. Stakes-Based Hooks
Format: Lead with consequence, not feature.
Examples:
- "In just 10 seconds, an AI agent founder lost control of his account — and the internet turned it into a $15 million scam overnight."
- "There's a social network where 1.6 million AI agents are talking to each other right now—and nobody knows what they're saying."

Why it works: audience immediately understands why this matters to them.

### 5. Counterintuitive Lesson
Format: "The real insight is X, not Y."
Examples:
- "The biggest risk isn't the tech. It's security, identity, and hype moving faster than reality."
- "Agents aren't replacements yet — they're power tools. And power tools without guardrails don't make you faster. They make mistakes faster."
- "In the AI space, attention and hype can grow much faster than actual technological progress."

Why it works: memorable one-liner takeaways get saved and reshared as quotes on Instagram.
